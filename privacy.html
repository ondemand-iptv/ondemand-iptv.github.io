Privacy Policy

OnDemand IPTV · Last updated 25 August 2026

OnDemand IPTV is a media player. It does not host, provide, index, or stream any channels, movies, or shows. All content you watch comes exclusively from a playlist or subscription you add to the app. We are not affiliated with any content provider.

Data we collect

OnDemand IPTV has no servers and no user accounts. We ourselves do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The only data leaving your device is handled by Google's advertising service on Android, as described below.

Data stored on your device

The following stay only on your device (in the app's local storage) and are never sent to us or to any third party controlled by us:

  • Playlist names and the M3U URLs or Xtream Codes credentials you enter
  • Your favorites and recently watched list

Deleting the app, or using "Sign out (remove all)" in Settings, permanently erases this data.

Network connections

When you play a stream, your device connects directly to the server address in the playlist you provided. Those requests do not pass through us. Any data handling on the provider's side is governed by that provider's own privacy policy, not ours.

Advertising

The Android version of OnDemand IPTV displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier and limited technical information (such as device type, general region, and IP address). This data is processed by Google, not by us — see Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in your device's Google settings.

We do not run our own analytics, and we never collect your playlist details, favourites, or viewing activity. The iOS version shows non-personalised ads only and requests no tracking permission.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
